# YouTube SEO

Optimize a video for search and suggested-video discovery, distinct from click-through packaging.

## What to produce

1. **Primary keyword/search intent** — identify (or ask for) the main term(s) viewers would actually search to find this video. Prefer specific, high-intent phrases over broad/generic ones.
2. **SEO title option** — a version of the title that naturally includes the primary keyword near the front, while staying click-worthy (this can differ from the packaging-focused title in `/yt-package`; note the tradeoff if so).
3. **Description**:
   - First 1–2 sentences: keyword-rich, natural summary of the video (this is what shows in search results and above the fold).
   - Followed by a fuller description, relevant keywords worked in naturally (no keyword stuffing), timestamps/chapters if available, and relevant links.
4. **Tags** — a short list of relevant tags: primary keyword, close variants, broader category terms, and any misspellings/alternate phrasings worth covering. Don't pad with irrelevant popular tags.
5. **Hashtags** (if platform-relevant) — 2–3 max, specific to the content.

## Rules

- Never sacrifice a clear, natural-sounding title/description for keyword stuffing.
- Ground keyword suggestions in what the video actually covers — don't suggest terms the content doesn't satisfy, which hurts retention and channel trust signals.
- If given existing metadata, do a gap analysis (what's missing/weak) rather than rewriting from scratch unnecessarily.
