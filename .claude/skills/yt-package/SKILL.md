# YouTube Packaging (Title + Thumbnail)

Packaging is the title and thumbnail working as one unit — they should never repeat the same information twice.

## Process

1. Get the video's actual content/premise and the core "big idea" or outcome.
2. Produce **8–10 title options**, varying the hook mechanism across them:
   - Curiosity gap ("The Real Reason...")
   - Numbered/list ("5 Ways...")
   - Direct outcome/stakes ("I Did X for 30 Days")
   - Contrarian/controversial angle
   - Question format
   Keep titles under ~60 characters where possible, front-load the interesting word, avoid clickbait that the video doesn't deliver on.
3. For each strong title, propose a **matching thumbnail concept** that adds NEW information the title doesn't state — e.g., title poses the question, thumbnail shows the visual proof/result/emotion. Describe:
   - Main subject/framing (face + expression, or object)
   - Background/setting
   - Any text overlay (2–4 words max, high contrast)
   - Color/contrast strategy for feed visibility
4. Flag the single best title+thumbnail pairing as the recommendation, with a one-line reason.

## Rules

- Never suggest a thumbnail that just restates the title in text form.
- Never suggest a title that oversells beyond what the content delivers.
- Favor specificity over vague superlatives ("best", "amazing") — concrete details drive clicks.
