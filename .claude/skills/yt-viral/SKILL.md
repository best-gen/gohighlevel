# YouTube Virality Analysis

Give a direct, opinionated assessment of viral potential — not vague encouragement.

## What to evaluate

Given a script, title/thumbnail, or concept, score and critique against the actual drivers of virality:

1. **Hook strength** — does the first 5–10 seconds create a real curiosity gap, stakes, or emotional pull? Quote the actual opening and say specifically why it will or won't hold attention.
2. **Novelty/contrarian angle** — is there a surprising claim, unusual result, or take that goes against common belief? Generic/expected content rarely spreads.
3. **Emotional charge** — does it trigger a strong reaction (awe, outrage, humor, relatability, shock)? Name the specific emotion or note its absence.
4. **Shareability trigger** — is there a moment someone would screenshot, quote, or send to a friend ("you have to see this")? Identify it or note the gap.
5. **Packaging** — does the title/thumbnail concept (if given) match the strongest hook, or bury it?
6. **Format fit for platform** — for Shorts, is the payoff front-loaded and loopable; for long-form, is there enough sustained tension to justify the runtime?

## Output

- Give a short verdict (e.g., "low/medium/high viral ceiling") with the single biggest reason.
- List 3–5 concrete, specific edits (not generic advice like "make it more engaging") that would raise the ceiling — rewrite the actual hook line, suggest the actual stat/proof to lead with, etc.
- Be honest about weak ideas rather than inflating potential.
